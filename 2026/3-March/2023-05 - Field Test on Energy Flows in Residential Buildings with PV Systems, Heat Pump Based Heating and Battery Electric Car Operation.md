## Overview

Degree of autarky: the ratio of self consumption to the total consumption of electrical energy as a percentage.

Quantifies the household electrical demand of 3 different energy consumers with a solar panel.
## Problem

Temporally fluctuating consumer load means that the consumer must interact with the power grid, which creates system instabilities. This load is split into 
- Heating
- BEV
- Household electrical demands

Increasing the level of autarky in a residential building through battery size optimisation by analysing load profile of the consumer.

## Limitations

Highlights that self consumption is not visible to the power grid operator and energy supplier, which is incorrect. The paper does not take a system level approach to the problem.

Also does not show the use of thermal batteries.
