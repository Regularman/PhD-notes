## Contribution

- Information aggregation service - smart meter
- Collaboration aware services - smart homes
- Ubiquitous services - smart city

The application of IoT devices is smart demand response, optimisation of electricity consumption, preventative analysis, and integration into larger processes, heat pump operation commissioning.
- It is noted that there are currently no business models for heat pump commissioning

There are many existing heat pumps that have smart meters implemented for preventative maintenance. Some of these companies may already have optimal take back times in mind.
- This may include live monitoring and early warning system for the heat pump performance
