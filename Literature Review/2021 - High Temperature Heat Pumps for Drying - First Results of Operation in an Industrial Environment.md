## Contributions

High temperature heat pump up to $160\degree C$ with a size of 400kW, as part of the Horizon 2020 Dryficiency project. The heat pump is tested in a brick drying facility in Wienerberger, Austria.

The heat pump is a twin cycle heat pump, and the condensers are connected in series. The condensation temperature of cycle 1 is lower than cycle 2 by about 10K, and the evaporators can be operated in parallel or series.

![[Screenshot 2026-08-04 112611.png]]

Due to sub-cooler on the condensation side, more heat can be transferred to the heating the air.
- The IHX is the internal heat exchanger, and the sub-cooler is between the condenser and the IHX. 
- The IHX works by transferring heat from the high pressure side to the low pressure side of the heat pump. This IHX is an economiser circuit that further superheats the low pressure side of the compressor, which allows the compression to get closer to the critical temperature.
![[Screenshot 2026-08-04 113640.png]]
Note that the IEA HPT Annex shows more than 300 application examples for industrial heat pumps.

- Focus was placed on material selection to ensure structural integrity at high temperatures
## Content

The drying air is heated by internal heat exchangers, supplied with water with $90\degree C$. The waste water then pumps through the heat pump and heats the drying air up to $160\degree C$, replacing a natural gas burners.
![[Screenshot 2026-08-04 112611.png]]
Installed measurement devices to check pressure and temperatures within the heat pump as well as air flow, pressure, temperature and moisture levels of the brick drying tunnel.
- The heat pumps were operated in stationary operation for 1-3 hours with different start up times, showing stable temperature outlet temperatures at constant inlet temperatures.

Furthermore, the paper also trialed the heat pumps at 4 different operation points of different inlet and outlet temperatures.
- Showed that the COP of the heat pump at different lift temperatures were in agreement with industry standards.
![[Screenshot 2026-08-04 113248.png]]
## Limitations

More work need to be done on the long term stability of the heat pump in high temperatures with its components such as refrigerants, lubricants, and compressors.
