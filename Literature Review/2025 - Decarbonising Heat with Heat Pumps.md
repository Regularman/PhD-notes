### New technologies

| Technology                                              | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| $CO_2$ heat pumps                                       | 500,000 units sold in 2018. Driven by government policy, installer training, performance standards, and financial incentives. The banning of HFCs and the pursuit of low GWP refrigerants have led to the development of $CO_2$ heat pumps. These systems also offer a higher COP than other HFC heat pumps. They can operate at $90\degree C$ while maintaining a high COP. |
| Sub-cooler                                              | Increase COP of heat pumps for low temperature applications. It uses an external heat sink to reduce throttling losses and refrigerant flow rate. However, the maximum temperature lift is still limited by the pressure ratio of a single compression stage.                                                                                                                |
| Water heat pumps                                        | Experiment shows COP of 4.9 from $80\degree C$ to $110\degree C$ and COP of 1.9 to $150\degree C$. Trials done with and without external sub-cooler and is sub-critical, single stage compression with setparator.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Two speed compressor                                    | Effective for zone control system for more flexibility of operations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Scroll compressors                                      | Refrigerants are compressed as the two scrolls rotate around each other. They are quieter compared to piston compressors and have a longer operating life and are quieter. They can provide $5.6-8.3\degree C$ higher temperature life compared to piston compressors.                                                                                                       |
| Oil free magnetic bearing centrifugal heat pump booster | Oil free magnetic bearing means that an EM field is used to levitate the bearings and rotate the compression shaft, eliminating mechanical friction and wear/tear.
