## Contributions

Evaluated the decarbonisation of a University in Turkey by three main pathways,
- Technical: which involves capital investment into insulation, retrofitting with more efficient heating technologies and installation of PV modules. More concisely, insulation can be the thermal insulation of walls and roof. Window replacement, additional shading devices, and upgrades to mechanical systems
- Operational: Installation of IOT devices for better energy managements
- Social: Improving behavioural patterns with energy consumption
## Content

Total system cost is sensitive to replacement rates as well as end of life scenarios.

Note that technology implementation and usage behaviour are inherently decoupled. Just because we install low emissions technology doesn't mean that they will be used.

Outlines the need to rationalise the payback period of cost and carbon emissions. And in the conclusion of the study, the authors the highlights that although the economic pay back period is $7.1$ years, the carbon emissions payback period is $0.8$ years.

Public infrastructure has a much higher tolerance for payback period compared with private industries, and $7.1$ years for the financial payback period is deemed acceptable.
