What are the levers that drives energy demand reduction in the absolute sense? In the context of my PhD research, it is important to understand how heating networks will influence consumption behaviors of the end users through rebound effects, economic growth, and changes in consumption patterns. 

#### Economic Growth and Energy demand

There is no strong empirical evidence that shows that economic growth is being decoupled from energy consumptions  [[2019 - Decoupling Debunked - Evidence and Arguments against Green Growth as a sole strategy for sustainability]]

Therefore, it is fair to say that energy consumption will continue to rise in the future in a growth oriented economy. Especially in the industrial sense, as demands for commercial products increasing, the manufacturing capacity will increase. Within a national boundary, this means that the demand for heat will increase.

- If this is taken to be an immutable truth, then the only way in which heating systems can reduce demand is through increased energy efficiency of a centralized system versus a decentralized system. 
#### Energy efficiency and energy demand

There are two reasons why it is difficult to correlate increased energy efficiency with decreased energy demand. To make thus claim, we have to carefully define the spatial and temporal boundaries (for example heat pumps are more efficient than gas boilers in the operational phase, but this may be different on a lifecycle basis)
- It also necessitates the invocation of all else being equal assumptions.

Further, energy demands (for heat in particular) are also due to internal air temperature, radiant temperature, air velocity, humidity, activity levels, clothing, external temperature, behavioral norms, and socio-cultural norms. It is not just the converter efficiency and insulation of the building envelop that affects energy demands.

There is also the phenomenon where cheap heat leads to growth, although this effect may be diluted as heat is a secondary product, and manufacturing cost are also a product of the fluctuating global market place.

[34] Highlights that California's electricity consumption has remained constant since the 1970s and is 40% below US average on a state level, but only 1/3 of this is due to energy efficiency policies.

#### Energy market and energy demand 

Although energy demands are largely responsive to price, they are also dominantly shaped by sociopsychological factors. Notwithstanding the fact that most players do not have sufficient information, and incentives to respond to price signals, it does not mean that they are able to. Energy costs then becomes secondary to inertia due to energy-using habits.

- The ability of individuals to act is affected by the diffusion of technology in society or the economy. How does a heating network enable a more rapid diffusion of technology?
