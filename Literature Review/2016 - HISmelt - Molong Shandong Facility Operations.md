Partnership with Rio Tinto in Kwinana before being moved to China in the Shandong Province.

Uses a Smelting Reduction Vessel (SRV) that operates at $1400\degree C$. Iron ore fines, flux and coal are dropped into the SRV to produce pig iron. [Source 1](https://scindeks-clanci.ceon.rs/data/pdf/proc-0055/2025/proc-00552504281H.pdf) states that 850kg of fuel is needed to make 1t of hot metal. This agrees with  [Source 2](https://onepetro.org/aistaistech/proceedings/AISTECH19/AISTECH19/AIST-PR-377-063/802480?searchresult=1) which highlights that 900kg of coal is needed per tonne of hot metal. There are opportunities to replace coal with biochar to make the process net zero in carbon emission. Hot air blasts at $1200\degree C$ supplies oxygen to post combust the off gas inside the SRV. The combustion of off gas transfers down to the slag layer through slag droplets, and transfer their heat towards the endothermic smelting of the hot metal. Note that type of feedstock matters. Hematite will produce approximately 80 tonnes per hour at $800\degree C$. Magnetite, which decomposes into hematite in an exothermic reaction, will produce 100 tonnes per hour of hot metal, and DRI, which is pre-reduced, will produce 200tph of hot metal.

The Shandong plant owned by Molong produces 500,000t of hot metal per year for their BOF and has been operating since 2016. ~={red}This is to responds to China's closure of all small BF under 1Mt for environmental reasons.=~ The Shandong facility preheats and dry the hematite in a rotary kiln to $200\degree C$ and then a second rotary kiln to $800\degree C$ via the combustion of pulverised coal. The addition of coal mixed with the ore will pre-reduce the ore. There is also option to feed cold ore into the SRV. The hot blast oven is fueled by offgas that is not combusted. The remained of the offgas goes to a CHP turbines. The limitation of the HIsmelt process is that is operates under aggressive smelting conditions and requires frequent maintenance to replace the refractory lining [Source 2](https://onepetro.org/aistaistech/proceedings/AISTECH19/AISTECH19/AIST-PR-377-063/802480?searchresult=1).
