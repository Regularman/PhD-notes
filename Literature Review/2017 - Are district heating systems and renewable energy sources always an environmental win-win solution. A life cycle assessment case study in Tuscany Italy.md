## Content

Questions the assumption that district heating is an optimal solutions, offering an analysis of how to best deliver heat to consumer from a first principles approach.

Conducts a cradle to grave analysis of 3 different heating systems for a residential neighbourhood of 1000 inhabitants in Tuscany, Italy, serving sanitary hot water, space heating and cooling
- Geothermal heat pump
- Biomass system
- 2 other systems based on natural gas as a fossil fuel

Looks at technology and standalone vs distributed applications.
