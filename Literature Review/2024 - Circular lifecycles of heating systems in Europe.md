## Contributions

This work contributed to literature by providing the 
- statistics on heating infrastructure that exists in existing buildings from 2000 to 2020, as well as key heat systems up to 2045
- material components of these heating systems
- review on manufacturers and their EOL policies and strategies
- the study also looked at various sustainability assessment system in regards to heat supply systems
## Content

Contains information about the material distribution of various heat pumps (air to water, air to air, water to water) heat pumps at various sizes.
