## Oil and gas Refinery

The number of oil and gas refinery has decreases from 8 to 4 from 2004 to 2019. 
- Perth (Kwinana)
- Melbourne (Altona)
- Geelong
- Brisbane

The paper should avoid discussing of investment in renewable energy facilities for natural gas processing as society is trying to shift away from these fuels. Furthermore, investment in these facilities will displace potential for biofuels to come into play for other technologies.
- Main emissions is through the combustion of these natural gas. Electrification of transport, and the use of drop in fuel will be essential to shift demand away from the sector.

These plants primarily use natural gas as fuel for medium temperature processes
- crude oil distillation 
- thermal cracking of heavy hydrocarbons
- (removing sufficient CO2 to meet the pipeline quality for domestic gas or LNG). Natural gas from different gas fields have varying level of CO2. For the production of LNG, the feed to liquification must have close to zero CO2 content to avoid it freezing within the process. This is done through amine absorption where the scrubbing material is regenerated by heating to around $120 \degree C$ to drive off the CO2. This is usually provided by the waste heat from compressor turbine.

However, the report states that the use of gas means that more of the hydrocarbon derived from the crude oil can be converted to higher value petroleum products. 

- Natural gas is also consume in gas turbines at production facilities that drives compressors for pipeline transport. But this is not a heat process and therefore not within the scope of this study. However, are there opportunities here to switch to CHP?

When looking at natural gas and oil manufacturing, need to account for the decrease in demand due to shift away from fossil fuel. (What is their projected production capacity going into the future?)
