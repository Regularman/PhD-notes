## Contribution

Aims to demonstrate solar hydrogen at above 15% conversion efficiencies. Currently transitioning from lab-=scale prototype to market ready product.
- Stability is also aiming to be above 250h
- Pilot plant should produce 1000 $kgH_2/day$ and a path to a LCOH of less than $\$1/kgH_2$ 

Electrode and stack development with Fraunhofer, and module development with Lawrence Berkeley

By November 2026, they should have engineering design of a DSTH pilot plant
- Followed by techno-economic assessment and market assessment
- By 2029, they are aiming to a conceptual design of a DSTH manufacturing plant
- They are trying to advance it from TRL3 to 6.

Total project cost of 7.52M and ARENA funding 2.25M.
## Content

The point of this technology is that it directly integrates solar cells with catalytic electrodes, bypassing the energy conversion stage and enabling a high levelised cost of hydrogen.

As of 2025 interim report
- Electrode and stack development has began
- Module development has began
- System development has began

Demonstrated a 25x25 $cm^2$ DSTH module with solar to hydrogen efficiency of 17% under natural sinlight
- Demonstration of a larger ize electrode assembly preparation by up-scalable processes
- Currently transitioning direct solar energy generation of hydrogen to a commercial scale
- Testing under realistic operation condition is int he works
- Early stages of pilot plant design

Barriers with upscaling -
- Size of the electrode is an issue, as the labs needs a large container to hold the electrolyte and the 50x50 $cm^2$ electrode. They got around this by performing deposition separately on each piece of the electrode. It is hard to ensure uniformity on a large surface area.
- Unsure how the assembly of multiple electrode segments will affect long term selectro-chemical performance
- Natural sunlight was used rather than a solar simulator due to limitation of the size of the simulator and long lead time with larger equipment. However, this opens testing up to seasonality and weather,

Note that the team also needs to find a novel earth abundant high efficiency and stable catalytic material for the electrode stack
