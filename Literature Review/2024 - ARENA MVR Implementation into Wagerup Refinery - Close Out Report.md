[[2022 - ARENA MVR Retrofit and Commercialisation Report - Wagerup refinery]]

Commenced in 2021 and continued until 2023, when it was found to be financially unfeasible. It no longer met the objective of low capital form of evaporation.

- Iterative design meant that design hours and equipment required grew substantially. Ultimately did not fulfill the demonstrative purpose of the project.

Planned the installation of 2x 2MW low speed MVR compressor in series that would double waste vapor pressure form 80kPaA to 160 kPaA. An unused Falling film Evaporator was recommissioned for this purpose.
- 56tph evaporation
- Legacy asset being refitted means that the heat exchange area was suboptimal. Deisgn is a tradeoff between heat exchnage area and power consumption of the compressor

Water vapor from the evaporator has to be cleaned before entering the compressor.

Wagerup is located in close proximity to firm renewable energy, which is ideal.

## Packages commenced

- Compressor package
- Silencer package
- Valves were long lead time and approved
- Refit of the Falling Film Evaporators

## Issues

- Required dedicated relief line as existing relief line to the evaporator feed would not conform to AS1210, and steam relief would not be able to be handled by evaporator feed tank vents
	- Furthermore, acid wash operating pressure would causse exothermic reaction in the evaporator feed tank.
- Accumulation of hydrogen during acid washing, and the velocity in the acid return line is high at 5m/s, which can cause potential to wear. This required increased diameter of the acid vent line.
- Inadequate scrubbing of vapor. Quantities of condensed vapor, de-superheating spray water will accumulate in fans and suction silence, causing the fans to trip. This required additional condensate removal system.
- There are also additional problems with the flash condensate during start up, caustic washing overpressure, insufficient steam line and lack of steam flow meter to calculate evaporation economy, poor liquor main ring control
- The current trial also does not allow start up with no steam addition, and is difficult to bypass line in the refinery layout. There are also high pressure losses in the main line.

Due to these issues, the project scope was closed and the design was never fully completed. This was as the project could not meet the low capital modular form of evaporation.

The design risks encountered were blamed on delay in issuing the scope of work, delays in supplier organization. The former was driven by additional iterative design cycles.

## Final Lessons learnt

- Insufficient rigours in gate reviews
- Need to conduct probabilistic analysis to account for discrete risk events in $P_10-90$ scenarios.
- Multiple contractors complicated supply chain. Preference of comprehensive supply chain is reccommended.
- Don't assign a part-timer to the project
