The standard set out the process for organisations and auditors to follow when undertaking energy audits to identify opportunities for cost effective investments to improve energy performance.

There are $3$ defined types of energy audits. this standard helps the practitioners know which method is the best.
- highlights reporting requirements

#### System scope

Energy is consumed on industrial sites for the foloowing

- Control of raw material (extracting, receiving, storage, and handling)
- Conversion processes (handling, processing, distributing)
- Finished products (receiving, storage, dispatch)
- Utility supply, waste stream, or by product treatment (waste water, gases, liquids, and solids), and disposal within or external to the site
- The utilities supporting these operations
