Research at the lab scale investigated several components that can unlock the high temperature electrolysis of iron ore. By doing this beyond the melting point of iron, we can use iron ore of lower grade as the impurity will be exported out of the slag.

The lab scale trials conducted by IERO looked at

| Field of Research                                                                                                                                             | Findings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Iron electrochemistry - what are the thermodynamics and kinetics of iron electrolysis at high temperatures? This is done in both molten salt and molten slag. | Through cyclic voltammetry, found that cathodic reaction is reversible as the reaction varies proportionally to $v^{1/2}$, which is the scan rate of the voltammetry test. and limited by diffusional transport.                                      |
|                                                                                                                                                               | Further, at low concentrations and low temperature, the main contributor to the cathodic reaction is ferrous metal ($Fe^{2+}$) at high temperature and high concentration, while $Fe^{3+}$ is the main reactant at low temperature and concentration. |
|                                                                                                                                                               | Ionic conduction in the molten slag prevails compared to electronic.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
