Research at the lab scale investigated several components that can unlock the high temperature electrolysis of iron ore. By doing this beyond the melting point of iron, we can use iron ore of lower grade as the impurity will be exported out of the slag.

| Development of anode that can withstand corrosive nature of molten salts at high temperatures, whilst maintaining high electrical conductivity. Trying to seek stability between redox stability and high temperature electrical conductivity. Iron oxide substituted spinels have been studied as possible anode materials. They are substituted magnetite compounds with magnesium, aluminium, titanium, chromium, manganese, and nickel. Their properties as refractory materials and conductive electrodes have been measured. | By using a magnetite electrode, the dissolution of $Fe_3O_4$ will not contaminate the system, but account for a fraction of the iron oxide raw materials. However, the melting point of magnetite is only slightly above that of metallic iron. Therefore, partial substitution of iron with magnesium is able to improve refractory effects, but decreases electron conductivity. While addition of alumina increases electron conductivity but have the opposite effect on thermal stability. |
|                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Thermochemical stability reduces dramatically when the anode is exposed to air due to the oxidation of the magnetite phase into hematite, causing contraction as $Fe^{3+}$ is smaller than $Fe^{2+}$. Therefore, tolerance against oxidation can be improved through substitution of iron with magnesium                                                                                                                                                                                        |
