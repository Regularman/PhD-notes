## Contributions

Current evaluation of project financial feasibility is based on assumptions of constant discount rates. However, the paper highlights that the discount rate is a complex figure that encapsulates macroeconomic factors such as inflationary processes and changes in exchange rate. Further, it considers microeconomic factors like investor preference for the distribution of consumption over time, the risk of asset impairment, and the risk of lost profits.

Therefore, the constant discount rate assumption is only valid in stable economies, and the NPV does not provide an accurate view on the cost of capital in emerging markets.
- Further, the risk level is entirely different at the start of the investment and at the end of the process. This assumption should be taken into account in determining the discount rate over time.
