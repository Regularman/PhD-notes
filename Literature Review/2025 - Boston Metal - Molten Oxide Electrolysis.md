## Pilot plant - 2018 to 2023

The end goal of the pilot plant is to demonstrate the stable production of iron and oxygen. It moved the TRL from lab scale testing of inert anode and pilot cell to long duration operation of the pilot cell.

1t per month scale facility in the pilot plant. 

- In the prior budget period, the plant produced a 10kg batch of liquid steel
- In 2023, the plant was able to produce a 100kg batch of liquid steel [Source](https://www1.eere.energy.gov/iedo/downloads/2023/peer_review/Boston%20Metal_Lambotte_IEDO_Iron-and-Steel_Carbon%20Free%20Iron%20for%20a%20Sustainable%20Future.pdf)
- In 2025, the plant was able to produce a 1t batch of liquid steel [Source](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/05/20/1137523/boston-metal-funding-critical-metals/)

The main development is a chemically inert anode design that can operate under the corrosive environment.

Currently looking to expand to a demonstration project that will be operational and commissioned by 2028.
