## Contribution

Conducted literature review on PV/T collectors shows that hybrid PVT collectors can enhance energy utilisation. The key considerations are storage sizing, system dynamics, climate adaptability, and architectural integrations.

Work still needs to be done in cost-competitiveness and control optimization,
- Integration with heat pump systems
- Comprehensive life-cycle performance assessments of next generation solar assisted technologies.

Conducts analysis in the domestic use case. Does not show their integration into existing district heating systems.
## Content

Solar fractions of PVT systems can reach 80%, leading to significantly improved energy efficiency and reduced carbon emissions.
- New materials such as phase change materials can improve thermal energy efficiency and increase overall performance of the system. Improvements in the 4E (energy, exergy, economic, and environmental) is a good framework of looking at the impact of these systems. [3]

Scheduling algorithm using genetic algorithms and particle swarm approach to schedule the electrical and thermal components of PV-T systems. 

Matlab/simulink is used for simulation.
