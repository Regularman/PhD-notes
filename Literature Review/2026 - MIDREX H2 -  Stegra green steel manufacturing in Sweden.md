The DRI will be melted in an electric arc furnace before being processed through continuous casting, cold rolling, and galvanizing lines to produce the green products.

- Lower carburisation in the DRI might mean higher electricity consumption in the EAF.

There are opportunities to switch methane to biogenic methane. Hot DRI will be moved to EAF through 800 of underground piping network. The area also has a very high level of renewable electricity (almost 100%).

Hydrogen is more endothermic compared to carbon monoxide. Conventional plants that uses hydrogen have a CO percentage of 1.5 to 1.55. To mitigate the higher cooling rates, the FB must have higher temperatures or higher reducing gas flows. However, in this case, the temperature is fixed at $950\degree C$ and the reducing gas flow rate was increased (which will increase the thermal mass of the incoming gas flow).

Compared to other DRI process, there are higher water vapors in the off-gas, which increases the condensation heat load in the scrubber.
- Furthermore, there is no gas reformer anymore where expansion can take place (as electric heaters are used), so there is only one top gas scrubber to clean and cool the top gas.

Pressure swing adsorption is used to produce high purity hydrogen by dumping out nitrogen that has infiltrated the processed.
- The PSA tail-gas can be used for district heating

Third stage compressors are used for process flexibility and to manage the higher hydrogen flowrates, as well as the lower molecular weight of the gas.

Uses MIDREX-H2 to produce DRI. The plant will be supplied by a 800MW green hydrogen electrolyser. Also uses 15 Midrex tutco electric heater that is 13.7 MW each to reheat the hydrogen (total 250.5MW). Furthermore, there is an EAF used to process the DRI.
## Product

HDRI is provided at $600\degree C$ to the EAF. Reduces specific energy by 130-150 kWh/t-ls, and reduces electrode consumption by 0.5-0.6 kg/t-ls. Saves refractory consumption by 1.8-2 kg/t-ls. This also reduces tap to tap time by 20%.

The plant has also electrified their annealing and galvanising furnaces. [Source](https://www.sms-group.com/plants/projects/stegra)
