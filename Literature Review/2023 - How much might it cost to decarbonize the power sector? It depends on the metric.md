## Contributions

Compares $8$ different cost metrics for 2 base scenarios and 4 development scenarios to show how the choice of cost metrics can change the narrative of the decarbonisation of the power sector.

## Content

#### Net Present Cost

The net present cost is the discounted value of revenue minus the capital expenditure required to build the network and the operating expenditure of asset operation.
