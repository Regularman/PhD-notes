## Contributions

Compared the use of a Weight Linear Combination MCE method, Kepner-Tregoe decision framework and CommunityVIZ (open-source GIS tool) for the evaluation of site selection using location specific variables for a trail gasification plant in Wyoming, US. The 3 tools used were
- A PSS based state wide suitability assessment
- Evaluation of site proposals offered by local governments and private entities through an RFP
- Parallel evaluation of 6 decision options using both PSS at the parcel level and the K-T method

Overall, this shows how location specific factors can be used to determine site suitability. However, in the application of this PhD, the decision boundary for if a heat networks should be implemented is unclear. Therefore, we must establish the correlation between each location specific variable and the outcome of the system, which is the point of my study.
- Further, variables such as heat density and heat diversity is inherently coupled, rather than independent. This means that the decision variables may form a non-linear decision boundary? E.g. In this paper, proximity to the wetlands is always bad. However, in my case, dense heat load is good depending on the value of heat load diversity.
## Content

The location specific factors used were 
- proximity to highways, power, fuel, transportation, landfill, a municipality, hospital, fire department, rails, emergency services, and food and shelter, wetlands, threatened and endangered species
- air quality, water quality, public support, chemical supply, local climate.

One thing to note in the multi-criteria selection methodology is that the criteria selection and weighting process is often very time consuming and subjective. In my own study, I will have to address how the significance of each factor is decided.

They also found that removing/filtering the initial GIS data was useful in speeding up processing of the GIS based suitability analysis.

## Limitations

The criteria and weighting definition stage of the MCE process is manual and the result of months of discussion. What happens when the decision boundary is formed through an optimisation study?
