### Visy

4x beverage can sites across Australia and New Zealand. Making 582 million cans per year, part of which compressed air is very important.

This is in North Queensland.

1 year after a compressed air as a service.
- 37% energy savings
- New system is a High Pressure System (7bar) supplied by variable speed compressors and a low pressure system (4 bar), with series compressors. Identified that some part of the site only needs low pressure
- Old system put out a lot of heat, not air cooled, extremely inefficient.
- Their old system was not fit for purpose, and taking maintenance staff away from more important issues. 
- N+1 redundancy across compressors, dryers, and filtration. Installed smart air controls and IIoT-based iConn monitoring for pressure control, fault detection, and remote performance management. Taken away the need for maintenance.
	- Gone from constant maintenance cool to no interaction with the compressors. They need to focus on core activity on site, which is manufacturing beverage cans,

Visy pays a unit charge per unit of compressed air. They will then own the asset post lease term.
- They also provide data for ClimaTech for 6 months to get the baseline for system sizing

### CaaS in Singapore

End to end carbon neutral development. They want to achieve BCA Green Mark Platinum, and wants to achieve 50% energy savings. They are looking for 0.465kW/RT annual average system efficiency

- Provide a 20 year CaaS model to provide chilled water in a Design Build Own Operate Maintain over 20 years. (pay per use model with no minimum offtake)
- Installed HT and LT chillers, EMS, HE pumps, cooling towers
- Transfers risk of upfront capital and no service repayments based on consumption until the plant is operation.

The advantage is that they get a BCA Green Mark Platinum certificate.

Customer provides water and electricity and JCI provides the chilled water.
