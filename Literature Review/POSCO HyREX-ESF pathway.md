## 2021 - Vision for POSCO Carbon Neutrality by 2050

The commercialisation of HyREX and EAF is not going to happen until 2050. In the mean time, the company is looking to inject natural gas and syngas into BF, new EAF to mitigate carbon emissions by 2040.

- Pilot processes by 2030
- Demonstration plants to be operating by 2040
- Commercialisation to be slated for 2050
