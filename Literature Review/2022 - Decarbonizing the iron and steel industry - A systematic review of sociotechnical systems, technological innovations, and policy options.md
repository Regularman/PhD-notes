#### What is direct reduced iron?

Direct reduced iron wis when the iron ore in the form of lumps, fines or pellets have has the oxygen removed with hydrogen and carbon monoxide. Sources of carbon monoxide are natural gas, coal gas, and goal. (Use of NG reduces the need for coal and coke and associated emissions of sulphur oxides and carbon dioxides into the atmosphere)

- Direct reduction serves as an alternative to blast furnace and can operate at lower temperatures.

### Barriers

The barriers to the implementation of decarbonisation solutions are mostly economic 

- Cheap access to fossil fuel and lack of industry knowledge sharing prohibits implementation of solutions due to a lack of technical certainty, hidden costs, and low information quality.
- A lot of the steel current in use have a lot of time left in their life span (due to their applications in buildings and infrastructure), which creates a time lag in decarbonisation solutions.
