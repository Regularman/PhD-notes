## Contributions

Outlines the pathways of waste heat utilisation for data centers, outlining how it can be implemented as well as a back of the envelope LCA analysis on its integration to district heating. It is a good reference to consider the different levers when optimising waste heat from data centres, as it presents its own risks and opportunities.
