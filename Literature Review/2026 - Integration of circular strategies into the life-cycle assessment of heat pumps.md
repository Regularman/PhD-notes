
## Contributions

Considers several R-strategies (reduce, refurbish, remanufacture, repair, and recycling). In this operation strategy, the paper seeks to look at the LCA of heat pumps and their refrigerant using the ReCiPe method.

The work is done for air-water heat pumps in environmental buildings.

| Scenario       | Description                                                         | Impacts |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| $S_{1M}$       | Mass reduction (Reduce)                                             |         |
| $S_{1W}$       | Heat demand reduction and mass reduction (Reduce)                   |         |
| $S_{3H}$       | Increasing lifetime from 17.5 $\rightarrow$ 18 years                |         |
| $S_{3S}$       | Increased maintenance intervals                                     |         |
| $S_{4}, S_{5}$ | Increased mass of the heat pump (Remanufacturing)                   |         |
| $S_{7K}$       | Recycling of refrigerants                                           |         |
| $S_{7M}$       | Increased collection rate and processing rate of metals (Recycling) |         |
Ultimately found that there is an increased environmental impact with increased maintenance (burden-shifting). This shows that we are displacing a problem rather than solving it.

The repair of heat pumps can be categorized into
- Hard faults: heat pump is no longer operational and repair can extend the lifetime of the heat pump
- Soft faults: heat pumps is operational but operating at a lower efficiency, these are identified during regular maintenance

Furthermore, remanufacturing involves the disassembling of products, then using those functional parts to make a new product. Refurbish involves returning the product to a like-new condition at the end of its life by replacing worn components.
- Both requires the heat pump to be repossessed by manufacturers or Service Providers
- Note that different heat pumps have different configurations, so a design is required to ensure that components can be easily accessed and replaced

Using the ReCiPe method, each scenario is measured based on the impact endpoints of 
- Human health
- Ecosystem quality
- Material resources
