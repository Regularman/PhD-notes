## Contribution

Primarily contributed to literature by suggesting the implementation of mushroom farms to the IS so that residual digestate from biogas production is not wasted. Biogas implementation in greenhouse was already explored, but faced issues of residual digestate management. This way, residual digestate can replace synthetic manure in mushroom growing to reduce acidification impacts.
 
After they commented on the GHG impact of a single case, commented on potential BC wide impacts if it was implemented.

Looked at the base case, case where biogas is produced and residual sent to mushroom farm, and where biogas is produced and residue is treated as waste
## Content

You first need to model the digester system, with all data coming from Eco-Invent data bases, industry interview, or reliable data sources online.

You can then use SimaPro to show the impact categories and characterisation method.

Normalised impact relative to economic output of the industry with the total economic output of British Columbia, but does not address the absolute planetary boundary.

Useful to be referenced for framework of IS LCA analysis by scaling up.

### Results

Showed that the production of biogas from Anaerobic Digestion reduces GHG emissions  and reduces the use of non-renewable energy. This is caused by the replacement of natural gas with biogas created locally. Carbon emissions come from fuel and energy consumption for digester system
